About Lesotho

Lesotho is one of the smallest countries in African continent, a country landlocked by South Africa

Lesotho is a mountanious counry worth exploring. Most of the land is high mountains. It is 1000 meters above sea level.That is why to some it famously called. THE KINGDOM IN THE SKY.
